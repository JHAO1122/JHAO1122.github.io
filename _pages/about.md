Self Introduction
====

Welcome to my personal website!

My name is Jiahao Tian and my nickname is Tommy. I'm a third year undergraduate student from [School of Mathematical Sciences](http://www.math.zju.edu.cn), [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn), majoring in Statistics. My research interests include Statistical Inference, Causal Inference, Machine Learning and Biostatistics. And I am also interested in other fields such as Bayesian Statistics, Stochastic Analysis, Quantitative Finance.

Starting from March 2025, I have an SRTP project under the guidance of a PhD student of [Prof.Xiaoye Miao](https://person.zju.edu.cn/miaoxy_cn), and our topic is about Multimodal Learning, Causal Inference and Missing Data Imputation. We always hold a group meeting once a week or once two weeks to discuss our research progress.

I'm also very fortunate to be advised by [Prof.Lijun Wang](https://hohoweiya.xyz/) of lab from School of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University. Now I am currently conducting research related to statistical inference, specifically on the Knockoff method for controlling FDR. We hold a group meeting every Thursday, mainly to discuss some interesting papers and the book ESL, as well as to share recent research progress.
