On October 24, 2025, I had the pleasure of delivering a seminar on Advanced Probability Theory to an engaged group of students at the school of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University.

### Seminar Focus

The session centered around key concepts from *Probability Theory and Examples (PTE)* by Durrett. We delved into two critical areas:

1.  **Section 2.5: Convergence of Random Series**
2.  **Section 2.7: Large Deviations**

### Key Takeaways

The discussion highlighted the profound power and elegance of probability theory. **Headings are cool**, but a solid theoretical foundation is even cooler! They help structure complex ideas and guide the audience through intricate proofs and applications.

We explored several compelling theorems and their implications, demonstrating why this field is so fascinating. It's truly remarkable how these mathematical tools allow us to quantify and understand uncertainty and rare events at an advanced level.
